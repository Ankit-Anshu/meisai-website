Why Meisai exists

Productive work breaks when its context is scattered

The problem is not a lack of apps. It is the gap between what you plan, what you save, what you learn, and what you eventually need to act on.

01

One workflow is split across too many tools.

Tasks, notes, saved pages, timers, learning plans, projects, and job applications often live in separate systems that cannot show the full picture.

Meisai connects them. The next action and its supporting context stay inside one browser workspace.

02

Saving information is not the same as understanding it.

Bookmarks and screenshots accumulate quickly, but they rarely preserve why something mattered or help you find the useful part later.

Meisai makes saved material usable. Read, highlight, comment, tag, search, and organize it by meaning.

03

Learning and career progress lose their history.

Resources, projects, resume files, applications, and interview notes become disconnected just when you need to show what you know.

Meisai preserves the progression. Learning paths lead into projects, job tracking, interviews, and resume versions.

04

The browser supports work and interrupts it.

Your most useful tools are often several clicks away while distracting websites are always one click away.

Meisai works at the point of need. Use quick actions, the command palette, Pomodoro, and focus blocking without leaving the browser.

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before you install.

Need something else? Visit the Help & Support page or send feedback directly.

What can I manage with Meisai?

Meisai brings tasks, calendars, habits, notes, saved reading, knowledge, focus sessions, learning paths, projects, jobs, and resumes into one connected workspace.

How does the command palette work?

Press Ctrl/Cmd + K from any Meisai page to find saved content, navigate features, create entries, open a URL, or search the web.

What is the difference between Read Later and Reading Mode?

Read Later organizes articles you want to return to. Reading Mode presents an article in a focused layout with themes, progress, highlights, and comment notes.

Why does Meisai need access to webpages?

Supported-page access powers the visible Meisai Helper, user-requested Reading Mode, screenshot capture, and user-configured website blocking. See the support page for a permission-by-permission explanation.

How does website blocking work?

Add distracting websites in Settings. Meisai blocks those sites while a Pomodoro focus session is active and supports controlled five-minute breaks.

Which browser are supported?

Meisai supports all Chromium-based browsers, including:Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Opera, Brave.
